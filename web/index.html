    Lights

    Off holds a light dark until you turn it back on; on returns it to its schedule. The Prime HD stays off even with this PC off. The 16HD stays off while the dashboard runs, and for about 16 hours after it closes, then goes back to its schedule.

    Live control

    Moving a slider takes over the selected lights, and they stay that way until you press "Back to schedule" or use a light's on/off switch, even with this PC off. (The Prime HD holds by itself; the 16HD holds for about 16 hours without the dashboard, then goes back to its schedule.)

    Schedule

    Points

    Levels in %. Moonlight is used by the Prime 16HD only. Changes save as a draft automatically; nothing reaches the lights until you send it.

    Shift

    Negative is earlier, e.g. −2 starts the whole schedule two hours earlier. The points above keep their times; the Prime HD and Prime 16HD chart views show the shifted times. Takes effect when you send.

    Per-light tweaks

    Scale multiplies every channel; an offset adds or removes percentage points on one channel. Both only affect channels that are on, so dark periods stay dark.

    Dim mode

    70%

    Switch it on to run both lights' schedules at the level on the slider, at their usual times. While it's on, moving the slider changes the level on the lights; while it's off, nothing on the lights changes. It's written into the schedules on the lights, so it stays on, even with this PC off, until you switch it off (the 16HD can take up to a minute over Bluetooth).
